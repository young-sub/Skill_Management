# Human Readability Policy v3 (Dormant)

- Agent contracts and durable technical documents are English. Design and Result Review surfaces are Korean by default.
- Reviews use the same stable Item IDs and order, show What, How, Test, and Done, and explain unfamiliar terms on first use.
- Use a behavior-matched flow/state/migration visual. Do not expose raw Markdown, frontmatter, hashes, detailed logs, or audit appendices.
- Status always has text and an icon in addition to color.
