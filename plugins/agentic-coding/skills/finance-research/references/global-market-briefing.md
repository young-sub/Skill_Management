# Global market comprehensive briefing workflow

Use this reference for recurring or ad-hoc Korean global-market morning briefings, especially when the user asks for a Kim Hyun-seok / Wall Street Now style analysis.

## Core frame

Do not write a headline list. Interpret the market in this sequence:

1. **Market numbers first** — S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow, Russell 2000, Treasury yields, dollar, oil, gold, VIX.
2. **Economic data vs consensus** — CPI, PCE, payrolls, GDP, retail sales, PMI, housing, auctions, and Fed speakers. Absolute values matter less than surprise vs consensus and market reaction.
3. **Rates / Fed path** — translate every major news item into whether it increases or decreases cut/hold/hike expectations. Treat the 10-year yield as the key equity valuation pressure point; watch 30-year yields for deficit/supply concerns.
4. **Earnings and sectors** — connect earnings/guidance/CapEx to sector winners and losers, especially AI, semiconductors, cloud/big tech, AI power infrastructure, financials, industrials, energy, and consumers.
5. **Market internals** — check whether the move is narrow mega-cap tech leadership or broad participation through Russell 2000, equal-weight indices, financials/industrials, breadth, VIX, leverage/crowding signals.
6. **Risk scenarios** — explain risks by transmission path: event -> rates/inflation/margins/consumption -> valuation/sector impact.
7. **Next checkpoints** — CPI/PCE/jobs/FOMC/Fed speeches/Treasury auctions/earnings/oil/geopolitics.

One-line principle: analyze how news changes **rates, earnings, valuation, and sector rotation**, not the news itself.

## Preferred Korean report shape

```markdown
## 글로벌 증시 종합 분석 — [date/time 기준]

### 오늘의 결론
- 3–5 lines. State direction, main driver, and the market debate first.

## 1. 자산별 하루 요약
- S&P500 / Nasdaq / Dow / Russell 2000
- 10Y / 2Y / 30Y Treasury yields
- Dollar, WTI/Brent, gold, VIX
- Interpret whether equities, bonds, commodities, and FX confirm or contradict each other.

## 2. 왜 움직였나: 지표·Fed·실적
- Data vs consensus
- Fed comments / rate futures / cut expectations
- Major earnings, guidance, and CapEx comments
- Explain what the market priced in.

## 3. 금리와 밸류에이션 해석
- 10Y yield impact on growth/value/small caps
- 30Y / supply / auction issue if relevant
- Distinguish `good economy = earnings support` from `good economy = higher-rate pressure`.

## 4. 섹터와 주도주 흐름
- Select only the day’s relevant sectors.
- Link `CapEx increase -> beneficiary industries/companies -> pressured competitors`.
- Add Korean-investor implications briefly when useful.

## 5. 시장 내부 체력
- Narrow big-tech rally vs broadening participation
- Russell 2000, equal-weight, breadth, VIX, financials/industrials, liquidity/crowding.

## 6. 리스크 시나리오
- 2–3 risks as path analysis, not event lists.

## 7. 다음 체크포인트
- 3–6 items for the next day/week.

## 투자자 관점 한 줄
- One sentence on how to interpret the market and what to monitor.
```

## Data checklist

Use current tools/sources to verify at least the main levels and moves:

- US indices: S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite or Nasdaq 100, Dow, Russell 2000.
- Rates: 10Y, 2Y, 30Y Treasury yields. If live 2Y data is unavailable, say so briefly or use the best accessible source.
- Macro assets: WTI/Brent, gold, dollar index, VIX.
- Sector proxies when useful: QQQ, IWM, RSP/equal weight, XLK, XLF, XLI, XLE, XLY, SMH/SOXX, plus selected leaders.
- High-quality news/primary sources for why: Fed, BLS/BEA/Census/ISM, Treasury auctions, company earnings releases/calls, reputable financial media.

## User-specific preferences

- Korean polite/formal tone.
- Concise but sufficiently analytical; avoid long raw-news dumps.
- User cares especially about AI, semiconductors, Physical AI/robotics, AI power infrastructure, electricity/grid/data-center power/cooling, and medium-term event-driven momentum.
- Do not over-repeat mega-cap names as the main focus if the same names were recently covered. If unavoidable, treat repeats as short updates.
- Do not include notes about unused/excluded sources; mention data limitations only when they materially affect confidence.

## Pitfalls

- Do not confuse a risk-on day with a durable trend unless breadth, yields, and earnings support it.
- Do not say `yields fell` or `cut expectations rose` without checking current data.
- Do not make buy/sell calls. Frame as research, scenarios, watchpoints, and invalidation.
- Avoid source-name authority padding. The analysis should stand on the transmission mechanism.
