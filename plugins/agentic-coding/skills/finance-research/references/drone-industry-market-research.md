# Drone industry market research notes

Use this reference for Korean investment/research requests about drones, UAVs, C-UAS/anti-drone, military drones, industrial inspection drones, and drone delivery. Keep output split between **domestic Korea** and **overseas/global** when the user asks for 드론 관련 국내/해외 구분.

## Source hierarchy and reusable figures

### Korea / domestic

Primary sources to prefer:
- 관계부처 합동, `제2차 드론산업발전기본계획 (2023~2032)` — often mirrored by Korea Drone Business Association/FKDB as `제2차_드론산업발전_기본계획.pdf`.
- 항공안전기술원(KIAST), `드론산업실태조사` annual reports.

Key extracted figures from the session:
- `제2차 드론산업발전기본계획`: 국내 드론산업 규모는 2017년 1,999억원 → 2021년 8,406억원, 2032년 3.9조원 전망.
- Same plan: 세계 드론시장은 2021년 32조원 → 2032년 146조원, 연 14.9% 성장 전망.
- Same plan: 국내 전체 드론산업은 2027년 1.9조원, 2032년 3.9조원 예상; 배송시장이 전체 드론산업의 7~9% 전망.
- KIAST `2025 드론산업 실태조사`: 2024년 국내 드론산업 매출액 약 1조 1,107억원. 제작 4,654억원, 활용 6,423억원.
- KIAST 2025 세부 활용시장: 촬영·방송 2,402억원(37.4%), 측량·점검·관측 1,667억원(26.0%), 농업·방제 1,568억원(24.4%), 교육 및 기타 785억원(12.2%).
- KIAST 2025 global: 세계 드론시장 규모는 2024년 382억달러 → 2030년 578억달러 전망. Segment notes: 상업용 서비스 2024년 276억달러 → 2030년 약 427억달러; 하드웨어 61억달러 → 101억달러; 소프트웨어 16억달러 → 23억달러.

Useful domestic framing:
- Korea is still small and theme-sensitive; pure-play listed exposure is limited.
- Prefer defense/anti-drone, sensors/radar, military reconnaissance, UTM/low-altitude traffic management, public safety, and industrial inspection over consumer/camera drone themes.
- Domestic listed exposure examples: KAI, Korean Air, Hanwha Systems, Hanwha Aerospace, LIG Nex1, Firstec, Neontech, JC Hyun, PC Direct. Label small/theme names as higher volatility and validate actual drone revenue exposure.

### Overseas/global

Reusable sources and figures:
- Drone Industry Insights / DII market reports: useful for commercial/recreational split, regional/country market sizing, and bottom-up market methodology. Older public snippets: global drone market 2022 about USD 30.6B and forecast USD 55.8B by 2030 at 7.8% CAGR; newer product page is often paywalled but lists scope and methodology.
- MarketsandMarkets `Military Drones Market`: 2026 USD 34.85B → 2031 USD 109.22B, CAGR 25.7% (check current page because numbers may update).
- MarketsandMarkets `Counter-Unmanned Aircraft System (C-UAS) Market`: 2025 USD 6.64B → 2030 USD 20.31B, CAGR 25.1%.
- Grand View Research / PRNewswire delivery drones: global delivery drones market anticipated to reach USD 10.47B by 2030, CAGR 42.6% from 2023 to 2030. Use as high-growth but high-regulatory-risk figure.

Overseas company map:
- Defense/tactical UAV: AeroVironment (AVAV), Kratos (KTOS), Red Cat (RCAT), Northrop Grumman (NOC), Lockheed Martin (LMT), RTX, Boeing (BA).
- Sensors/thermal/inspection enablers: Teledyne (TDY), Trimble (TRMB).
- Civil/industrial/private leaders: DJI (private China), Skydio (private), Autel Robotics (private China), Zipline (private).
- Delivery/platform exposure: Alphabet/Wing (GOOGL), Amazon Prime Air (AMZN), Walmart partnerships (WMT).

## Preferred Korean output shape

For the user, produce a concise but evidence-backed structure:
1. `결론`: drone investment edge is not consumer photography; it is defense drones, anti-drone/C-UAS, industrial inspection, autonomous flight SW, and UTM/traffic infrastructure.
2. `국내`: market size, market structure, key growth points, Korean listed exposure, risks.
3. `해외`: global market size, defense/C-UAS/delivery submarket figures, global companies, risks.
4. `투자 관점`: separate stable defense primes from high-beta pure-play names.
5. `Source 발췌`: include short quoted/extracted source lines and source names.

Avoid Telegram tables; use bullets with labels.

## Pitfalls

- Do not treat Korean drone theme stocks as pure drone plays without checking revenue exposure.
- Do not over-index on delivery-drone CAGR; delivery is high-growth but depends on regulation, insurance, safety, route economics, and local permissions.
- Distinguish UAV/drone market scope: commercial/recreational excludes passenger drones and may exclude C-UAS; military drone forecasts are separate; C-UAS is a defense/security adjacency, not drone production itself.
- Use current source checks for market-size pages because market research vendors frequently update headline numbers and forecast windows.
