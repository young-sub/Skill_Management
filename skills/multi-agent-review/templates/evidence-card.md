# Evidence Note

Use only for a consequential or disputed claim that needs more detail than a short finding.

- Claim or failure mode and why it changes the decision.
- Primary evidence, whether inspected directly, and any inference.
- Strongest contradiction or remaining uncertainty.
- Resolution or falsifier; main-agent judgment when available.

Keep it in context unless a durable record is requested. Omit empty fields; confidence scores and severity labels are optional.
